## Three Sour D
3 Sour Diesel Autos hit the soil today, if things go well I'll be up to my neck in ⛽. Yesterday I started soaking the seeds in aloe water, the blended aloe is supposed to help the seeds, we'll see. I cut the bottom inch off solo cups, cleared a spot and put the cup upside down, then I filled them with some garden soil and planted the 3 seeds and covered with humidity domes. The solo cups were inspired by Grassroots bottomless pots. The Timber is lowered, dimmed, and Photone is reading 30 DLI at the center of the tent. I think this grow might go okay after totally fucking it up last time.

## Lemon Potion Update
I should have culled the Lemon Potion 2 months ago but my curiosity got the best of me. About a month ago it started to re-veg after a few weeks of stressed induced flower, it got really leggy and was throwing a lot of single leafs, signature re-veg symptoms. I switched the lights to 12/12 just to see what would happen and it did go back to flowering. After a few weeks of watching this gangly monster try to flower I put it out of its misery and cut it down. Every grow is a learning experience. 

After I chopped it I re-amended all the pots with Craft Blend, worm castings, and Bokashi Blend and let them sit in the dark tent for a week before starting the Sour D. As for the CBD experiment, I have a few beans of Meph's CBD strain, Canna-Cheese, I'll probably pop after the Hotz in the 2x2.
