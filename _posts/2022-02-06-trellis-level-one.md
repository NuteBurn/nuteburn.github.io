## Trellis Up
Spent a few minutes to set up the trellis, having all the ties and bamboo cut already made the process a breeze. I lowered it a bit and tucked the top under. The Trizzlers spread out so well I'm hoping that the single Hotz will fill the tent without a friend, time will tell.

## Aloe Water

Last week Buildasoil put a video out about aloe in the garden, its worth [checking out](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2GbNScfYf04). It inspired me to cut a piece off the aloe I have and blend it into water and feed it to the plant and soak the Soar D seeds. The Hotz seemed to like it so I'll probably try it again.
