## Rough Few Months
There's no fun in sharing struggles during a grow so I haven't been posting. A lot of hard lessons were learned and I'm hoping things are better for this third run. 

### Outdoor
Of the 4 plants that I tried to grow on the porch only 1, the Northern Lights, made it to harvest. I think I ended up pulling about 24 grams of flower off that plant. The Gorilla Glue got sick and never bounced back, the bag seed ended up being a male as well as the Bruce Banner that I started the outdoor season with. Better luck next year?

### Red Poison 
It was Foxtail City, I really struggled keeping these two ladies happy. I had issues with Homekit not turning on/off the lights when it was supposed to and I know that on at least one occasion they were left in the dark for 24+ hours during late veg/early flower and they didn't like that. I struggled a bit with canopy maintenance as well, I was more worried about manipulating the plants during flower than I should have been. At one point I had to super crop the main cola(during the flower stretch) on one of them to get it to stop touching the light.

In the end I had some really larfy, loose, and foxtailed buds. The yield was around 95 grams from those two plants, I kept some of the nicer top buds for smoking and used the rest, about 80 grams, for bubble hash. That was a fun little experiment, but it was a lot of work for only 1-2 grams of hash, I suspect my low yield was due to a bad grow and sub par genetics(these ladies were breed for <i class="purple">color</i>, not to dump hash).  I ended up pressing that into rosin and got just about 1 gram of rosin.
