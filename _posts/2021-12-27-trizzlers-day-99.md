This was my best looking plant to date, I haven't weighed it all out yet but I can tell its the highest yielder too. I credit that to the genetics and me finally figuring out how to control the canapy and a cheap way to measure the DLI I was exposing the plant to. Turns out I've been absolutely frying my plants, they were all getting way too much light and the [Photone](\notes#) app helped me figure that out.
